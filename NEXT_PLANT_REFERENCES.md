## Corrections to old baseline assumptions

- Spikeweed is a low **green thorn mat**, not an ochre mound. Spikerock is a low angular gray spike formation.
- Fume-shroom’s purple nozzle grows from its **cap**; Gloom-shroom is a round vented purple dome with only a narrow pale base, not a normal cap on a tall stalk.
- Kernel-pult’s actual face has drooping leaf-like brows; the old yellow moustache is an added project detail. Cob Cannon is a separate horizontal husk-and-corn cannon on a wheeled cart.
- Jalapeno stage 1 shares the label Ghost Pepper with a separate canonical plant/family. Keep this family an evolved chili, and do not substitute the white ghost-shaped plant.
- Winter Melon, Fume-shroom, Gloom-shroom, Cob Cannon and Spikerock have canonical distinct anatomy. Other named evolutions in this batch are project extensions, not evidence of separately existing game species.

## Puff-shroom (`puffshroom`)

**Stages:** Puff-shroom → Fume-shroom → Gloom-shroom

**Observed game anatomy**

- Puff-shroom: squat cream/pale-green stalk with flared flat ground base, oversized purple cap with irregular dark purple spots, two dark oval eyes and a small short pale-green trumpet on the stalk front.
- Fume-shroom: broad purple spotted cap with a prominent purple front nozzle growing from the CAP, not the pale stalk. Stocky pale-green stem carries dark eyes, sharply pinched brows and a frowning mouth.
- Gloom-shroom (PvZ1): rounded purple dome body ringed by short purple open vent tubes; two pale eyes look upward, tiny downturned mouth; only a narrow pale-green base peeks out below. It is not a conventional cap on a long stalk.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- All three stage names correspond to canonical distinct game plants. Use each exact identity; do not merely enlarge Puff-shroom.
- Fume is deliberately mad in project rules; Gloom can retain its characteristic melancholy/upward gaze without angry brows.
- All mushroom stalks/base structures meet the ground directly; never add shoes, feet, arms or a separate leaf pedestal.

**Production notes**

- Three canonical identities; match Puff/PvZ2, Fume/PvZ2 and Gloom/PvZ1 anatomy.
- Purple nozzle originates in cap at stage 1; vent ring replaces the single nozzle at stage 2.

**Handoff family rules:** Friendly, cute; no plant legs/feet; base leaves, roots, pad or pot; Stage 1 Fume-shroom mad; other stages friendly; Mushroom stalk flares to ground; no feet

## Sun-shroom (`sunshroom`)

**Stages:** Sun-shroom → Big Sun-shroom → Mega Sun-shroom

**Observed game anatomy**

- Golden yellow-orange domed mushroom cap with broad irregular orange-brown spots, cream short stalk widening into a continuous flat footless base. Two dark vertical oval eyes with white highlights and a gentle small smile. Cap dominates silhouette.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- Sun-shroom is canonical and grows larger in the real games. Big Sun-shroom and Mega Sun-shroom are project stage labels, not separately established canonical species.
- Extend the same cap/stalk proportions with increasing size. The old pointed sun-ray rim is a project embellishment, not a canonical Sun-shroom feature; keep cap recognizably mushroom-shaped.
- No feet, shoes, limbs or green leaf base.

**Production notes**

- Do not turn the final cap into a sunflower face or a floating sun.
- Support/cheer can use warm cap and stalk motion; separate sunlight effects belong to game renderer.

**Handoff family rules:** Friendly, cute; no plant legs/feet; base leaves, roots, pad or pot; Mushroom stalk flares to ground; no feet

**Inspected references**

## Cabbage-pult (`cabbagepult`)

**Stages:** Cabbage-pult → Cabbage Launcher → King Cabbage-pult

**Observed game anatomy**

- Round layered GREEN cabbage body sits on overlapping dark-green base leaves. Lighter ruffled outer leaves frame the face. Two black oval eyes with bright highlights and raised leafy brows; no separate mouth in inspected render. A long bent green leaf stalk arches back/up into a cup holding one cabbage payload.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- Only Cabbage-pult is canonical. Cabbage Launcher and King Cabbage-pult are project evolutions.
- Keep a layered cabbage body and leafy catapult at every stage; stronger arm, fuller leaf crown and restrained final crown are project progression.
- Do not turn the head into a generic pea or replace leafy lobber with a wooden siege cart.

**Production notes**

- Keep bent stalk attached to body through launch/recovery.
- The payload is a cabbage in a leaf cup, not a plain green ball; detached projectiles are handled by the game.

**Handoff family rules:** Friendly, cute; no plant legs/feet; base leaves, roots, pad or pot; Stays a cabbage

**Inspected references**

## Melon-pult (`melonpult`)

**Stages:** Melon-pult → Winter Melon → Mega Winter Melon

**Observed game anatomy**

- Melon-pult: horizontal squat oval GREEN watermelon body with pale wavy longitudinal stripes; dark oval eyes/brows on front and no mouth. Broad green base leaves. A short curled vine spring connects to a half-melon green bowl cradling an upright full melon payload.
- Winter Melon: same oval lobber anatomy in CYAN/ICE BLUE with darker blue stripes, blue vine coil and an icy jagged bowl rim; cool blue-green base leaves. No mouth in inspected render.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- Melon-pult and Winter Melon are canonical; Mega Winter Melon is a project evolution.
- Stage 0 must retain the project mad heavy brow and mouthless rule, even though this PvZ2 render has a milder brow. Winter stages are friendly.
- Preserve spring-vine and bowl geometry; final stage may enlarge icy facets rather than invent a humanoid king.
- No legs or feet. Body sits on leaves.

**Production notes**

- A normal round melon face without rear payload mechanism loses the canonical identity.
- Use cold blue/cyan material ramps at winter stages; avoid green recolour alone.

**Handoff family rules:** Friendly, cute; no plant legs/feet; base leaves, roots, pad or pot; Stage 0 mad and mouthless; winter stages friendly

**Inspected references**

## Kernel-pult (`kernelpult`)

**Stages:** Kernel-pult → Butter-pult → Cob Cannon

**Observed game anatomy**

- Kernel-pult: upright short GOLDEN corn cob with clearly separated chunky kernels; base wrapped in broad green husk leaves. Two small dark oval eyes beneath drooping green-brown leaf-like brows. A long angular green lobber stalk carries a cup and yellow corn-kernel payload. There is no canonical moustache in the inspected render.
- Cob Cannon (PvZ1): huge horizontal yellow CORN COB points forward from a green tapered husk casing with a narrowed stern eye. Body rests on a low brown wooden cart with two visible round pale corn-slice wheels. Organic corn/husk cannon silhouette, no face mouth.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- Kernel-pult and Cob Cannon are canonical. Butter-pult is the project middle-stage label; actual Kernel-pult can lob butter as an alternate projectile.
- Middle stage should keep corn cob/husk lobber anatomy and put a recognizable pale-yellow butter pat in cup; do not invent a butter humanoid.
- Final Cob Cannon is mad and mouthless; real round cart wheels are explicitly allowed. Use intact wheels, not leaf-shaped fake feet.
- Old yellow moustache description is a project addition, not required for actual-game resemblance.

**Production notes**

- Cob Cannon has a horizontal cannon profile; do not scale upright Kernel-pult and call it final stage.
- Use base cart and full round wheels in walking/attack/KO frames; no human legs.

**Handoff family rules:** Friendly, cute; no plant legs/feet; base leaves, roots, pad or pot; Stage 2 Cob Cannon mad, mouthless; real cart wheels allowed

**Inspected references**

## Squash (`squash`)

**Stages:** Squash → Super Squash → Mega Squash

**Observed game anatomy**

- Lime-green pear-shaped/squat gourd with a short thick cut green stem, lumpy shoulder ridges and small surface warts. Heavy deeply downturned brow forms one continuous menacing upper face; large off-white eyes with small dark pupils and a pronounced downturned lip/chin. Broad rounded flat ground base, no leaves or limbs.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- Only Squash is canonical. Super Squash and Mega Squash are project evolutions.
- All stages must look mad with heavy V brow while remaining comic/family-friendly. Increase shoulder mass, gourd size and strong squash silhouette; a final red headband is a project costume detail, not canonical anatomy.
- No arms, legs or feet.

**Production notes**

- Main attack should visibly compress/launch/pound the whole gourd body.
- Do not substitute a smiling pear or small green blob with feet.

**Handoff family rules:** Mad at all stages, cute not scary; no legs/feet

## Jalapeno (`jalapeno`)

**Stages:** Jalapeno → Ghost Pepper → Dragon Pepper

**Observed game anatomy**

- Glossy RED curved chili pepper, broad upper body tapering into a narrow hooked tip, short green calyx and long curled green stem. Bulging pale pink/off-white eyes with tiny pupils under an angry brow; wide gritted pale teeth and a projecting lower lip. No limbs or leaf pedestal.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- Canonical base is Jalapeno. Project stage labels Ghost Pepper and Dragon Pepper are not canonical upgrades of this plant.
- Important name collision: this game has a separate ghostpepper family. Do NOT replace Jalapeno stage 1 with the canonical white ghost-shaped Ghost Pepper; keep an evolved darker chili silhouette in this family.
- All stages mad. Dragon-like ridges/leaf fins can extend final red chili, but no full dragon head/body/legs.

**Production notes**

- Keep distinct curled green top stem, red pepper taper and gritted toothy face.
- No detached flame effects in character frame sources.

**Handoff family rules:** Mad at all stages, cute not scary; no legs/feet

**Inspected references**

- Jalapeno: https://pvsz2.ru/plant/52; local `PROJECT/art-src/pvz-ai/research/next-plants/jalapeno.png`.

## Cactus (`cactus`)

**Stages:** Cactus → Prickly Cactus → Spike Cactus

**Observed game anatomy**

- Tall upright ribbed bright-green cactus with two substantial raised branching cactus arms, clusters of long reddish-orange spines and a tubular forward shooting snout. Narrow pale eyes under flat slanted dark brow. Inspected PvZ2 render has a reddish-orange ruffled flower crown with three yellow stamens and a small brown stone ring at base.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- Only base Cactus is canonical; Prickly Cactus and Spike Cactus are project evolutions.
- All stages mad. Natural cactus branches are allowed (they are the plant, not human arms); no legs/feet.
- Earlier project uses pink flowers, which can be retained as a restrained flower color variant; the inspected actual reference uses warm red-orange blooms. Do not replace characteristic ribbed cactus/snouted silhouette with a generic round succulent.

**Production notes**

- Preserve long spines and flowering crown at 64px with readable clusters.
- Stage growth can raise branches and add ribs/spines/flowers without adding armor or unrelated crowns.

**Handoff family rules:** Mad at all stages, cute not scary; no legs/feet

## Spikeweed (`spikeweed`)

**Stages:** Spikeweed → Spikerock → Spike Titan

**Observed game anatomy**

- Spikeweed: extremely LOW flat oval GREEN weed mat, jagged leafy rim, numerous pale yellow tapered conical spikes distributed over its surface. Narrow yellow-green eyelids and tiny reddish-brown pupils near front; no mouth.
- Spikerock: squat wide GRAY/STEEL rock or metal-like mound with angular interlocking plates and large faceted spikes/horns. Yellow eyes with red pupils peer from jagged brow below central large spear point. Front has a low armored knuckle-like rocky ridge, no open mouth.

**Canonical and project-specific progression**

- Spikeweed and Spikerock are canonical; Spike Titan is a project evolution.
- Major baseline correction: base is a flat green thorn mat, not an ochre/brown hedgehog mound. Preserve a wide LOW silhouette; grow density and rock facets in later stages rather than turning it upright.
- Project rules say friendly for this family, unlike stern eyes in inspected PvZ2 render. Keep canonical ground/spike anatomy while softening eye attitude enough to respect project family rule.
- No arms/legs/feet.

**Production notes**

- Even the taller final rock spikes should emerge from a broad rooted ground mat.
- Do not add an exposed monster mouth or separate bipedal body.

**Handoff family rules:** Friendly, cute; no plant legs/feet; base leaves, roots, pad or pot
