# PVZ Garden — art research

Research date: 2026-11-11. Baseline is the supplied handoff archive. No production art is approved by this report.

## Coverage and evidence

141 plant families × 3 stages = 423 plant looks; 81 zombies; 12 bosses: **234 research rows, 516 looks**. Full source data (including all move descriptions) is retained in `research/game-data.json`. Every look was rendered through the main game’s loaded PX registry and visually reviewed in the dated contact sheets. The original `_all-art.html` and `_art-gallery.html` were also opened, but both omit `art-v2-ai*.js`; they show legacy art and cannot establish the current converted state. The main `index.html` does load the 36 converted families.

The original asset manifest contains 4,495 records and predates the current AI conversion. Preserve it as historical evidence; `ART_PROGRESS.json` adds current character status. Sheets 1–47 cover plants, 48–56 zombies, 57–59 bosses; 60–61 are correction sheets. References were matched by identity and sheet position, not filename alone.

## Applicable rules and document conflicts

The current user brief and ART_CONTRACT.md govern this job. Older procedural-art instructions (one owned file, old pastel style, 256-pixel illustrated loader and parallel helper ownership) describe previous passes and are superseded. No game publishing is authorized. Existing art remains a fallback. Save IDs, keys and save shapes remain unchanged.

The approved style board is a style reference. Its single-flower Queen and eyed baby Chomper illustrations do not override the explicit THREE-flower Queen and eyeless Chomper family rules. Similarly, the board’s dinosaur is not the legacy giant-zombie Gargantuar; preserve each data identity.

## Uncertainties and decisions for the sample

- Real iPad/iPhone performance, touch gestures, safe areas and audio are untested; desktop viewport checks are not a device test.
- The newer friendly-default rule conflicts with some old grumpy plant descriptions. Use friendly idle faces outside the named mad list; reserve temporary determination for action poses.
- The boss reference shows a cockpit-headed mech while the old Zombot has a giant zombie-head robot and a chest pilot window. Preserve the project identity when developing the sample, and present the difference explicitly for approval.
- Per-frame head metadata will specify logical head centre, eye line, radius and front; costume sizing is measured once at rest and only position follows the frame.
- Legacy scene callers supply mostly two-valued frame bits. Animation support must normalize time to a finite frame index before caching, and explicitly carry action progress for one-shot attack/fall/defeat clips. A runtime-only clock would be frozen by scene caches.
- First sample: Sunflower family (one/two/three flowers), Basic Zombie and Zombot. Requested total is 45 + 13 + 9 = 67 independently drawn frames. No full-set artwork before sample approval.
