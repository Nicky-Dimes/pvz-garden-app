# Actual-game art research

Direction confirmed by user on 2026-11-11: research actual Plants vs Zombies game art and redraw identities in our approved pixel style; simplified project bodies may be replaced. Apply this to plants and wider game art as well as bosses. Gargantuar and Sphinx-inator are approved. No further production approval is pending.

These references were downloaded and visually inspected. Observations below are our own. Image-search captions are not accepted as evidence without inspecting the image. Official promotion and licensed merchandise renders are stronger sources; community mirrors are secondary references. Reference files are development material under `_ref`, not runtime sprites. Runtime art is newly generated and indexed, not official images.

Sphinx official promotion cross-check: https://x.com/PlantsvsZombies/status/431528106768089088 (image https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Bf0YhtECEAAdiEm.jpg).

## Plants and wider art

The 141-family inventory in ART_RESEARCH.md remains a baseline checklist. It has not yet been externally researched family by family. Before each production family: inspect game art, identify silhouette and canonical features, then redraw distinct stages and actions in pixel style. Existing bow ties, toy proportions, generic faces and substitute bodies are not binding. For invented evolutions without a canonical equivalent, extrapolate the canonical family identity and preserve readable stage progression. Existing approved Sunflower progression has one, two, then three flowers. Keep save IDs and battle rules unchanged.

Production is incomplete. All 12 boss IDs now have 9 drawn frames (approved Zombot sample retained; 11 actual-game redraws). Sunflower and Peashooter each have 45 drawn frames. Basic Zombie has 13. Current total: 211 drawn frames. Eleven actual-game boss battles were opened in Chromium with no page errors or failed requests. Chomper and Wall-nut are the next production families; other families remain pending.

## Peashooter family

Inspected base Peashooter, uncostumed Repeater and original Gatling Pea images. Peashooter has one rear curl, black oval eyes, a single flared tube, curved thin stem and broad base leaves. Repeater keeps one tube and adds swept rear leaf crest and determined brows; it is not a double-barrel plant. Gatling Pea adds olive helmet, chin strap, black cheek stripes and four steel muzzle barrels. Removed the project bow tie. The first search result for Repeater wore a Viking costume; it was rejected as a base-identity reference.

References: https://www.clipartmax.com/middle/m2H7Z5i8H7m2Z5i8_peashooter-pvz-clipart-plants-vs-plants-vs-zombies-pea/ ; https://pvsz2.ru/plant/80 ; https://www.pngaaa.com/detail/412335 .

## Chomper and Wall-nut families

Inspected Chomper: purple spotted hinged jaw, green lip rim, irregular ivory teeth, purple curved stem, green leaf base/frill, no eyes. Actual base family: https://pvsz2.ru/plant/18 and licensed render https://walls360.com/plants-vs-zombies-chomper-iv/ . Super/Mega are project evolutions extending jaw size and frill naturally.

Inspected Wall-nut: lumpy oval golden-brown shell, ridged seam, large round off-white eyes. Tall-nut: tall rounded rectangular shell, heavy-lidded serious gaze. Keep project mouthless family rule while adopting the canonical shell anatomy. Giga Tall-nut is a project evolution with a low reinforcement band. References: https://pvsz2.ru/plant/110 and https://pvsz2.ru/plant/102 .
